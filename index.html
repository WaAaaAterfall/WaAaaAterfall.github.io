Hi, I'm Shiyang, and I also go by Lilian. I build generative models that find the quiet, low-dimensional structure underneath neural and behavioral recordings, and try to make that structure something we can actually interpret.

I'm a Ph.D. student at NYU's Center for Data Science, advised by John Pearson. I started my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Duke in 2024 and transferred to NYU with my advisor in 2026. Right now I'm studying how mouse motor cortex controls reaching. Now

Ongoing Nov 2025 – now NYU & Duke

Motor control in mouse reaching

Latent variable models of how mouse motor cortex controls reaching. I'm building a generative model that explains trial-to-trial variability through latent control signals, pairing a GP-VAE over the controls with flow matching to capture nonlinear, multimodal reach dynamics.

Earlier in the project, I used GLMs and Poisson linear dynamical systems to model how motor cortical output integrates distorted proprioceptive feedback.

  • GP-VAE
  • flow matching
  • motor control

Ongoing Feb 2024 – now Duke

Online latent models for calcium imaging

A state-space model, Glow-SPLAT, that finds low-dimensional population dynamics in streaming, large-scale calcium imaging data. It's fitted with a particle-filter EM strategy and written in JAX, and it keeps pace with thousands of neurons faster than the microscope records them.

  • state-space models
  • sequential Monte Carlo
  • EM
  • JAX
  • streaming inference

Earlier

Oct 2025 – Jan 2026 Duke

Dynamic compression flows

A dual flow-matching model that learns identifiable, low-dimensional latent dynamics from neural and behavioral data. One flow field models how the system evolves over time; the other maps the data into a low-dimensional latent space and is trained to leave those dynamics undistorted, with a nested-dropout constraint keeping the latent space small. The result is more interpretable dynamics and better reconstructions than competing models, even on noisy datasets where conventional methods fail. Published at ICML 2026.

  • flow matching
  • latent dynamics
  • dimensionality reduction
